FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Find quick answers regarding team composition, presentation requirements, hardware facilities, and certificates.

Teams must comprise between 2 and 5 members. Inter-department teams are fully encouraged! In fact, multidisciplinary teams combining computer science with electronics, mechanical, or biotechnology often score higher in feasibility and real-world execution.

Every student who presents their project in the preliminary and final rounds receives an official Certificate of Participation. Top winning teams across all 4 tracks will receive cash prizes totaling ₹1,50,000+, trophies, and potential seed grant incubation support from the Campus Entrepreneurship Cell.

100% of the Intellectual Property belongs exclusively to the student creators. The college and organizers make no claim over your source code, hardware designs, or patent rights. We also offer guidance on filing provisional patents through the College IPR Cell.
