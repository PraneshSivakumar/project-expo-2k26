02. ABOUT PROJECT EXPO

More than a competition,
a launchpad for innovation.

Project Expo 2K26 is an intra-college event designed to bring together passionate young minds to solve pressing challenges through technology, engineering design, and creativity.

Core Objective

Our Purpose

Project Expo is a platform for students to turn ideas into action. It encourages practical learning, creativity, and problem-solving by giving participants the opportunity to showcase their projects, share knowledge, and explore solutions to real-world challenges.

100% Student-Driven Fostering grassroots innovation across disciplines
Long-term Aspiration

Our Vision

To create a strong culture of innovation where students think beyond textbooks, challenge conventional ideas, and build solutions that make a meaningful difference in society.

Ideate Prototype Deploy Scale
Action & Impact

Our Mission

To empower students with opportunities to experiment, collaborate, and innovate. Through Project Expo, we aim to strengthen technical skills, build confidence, encourage entrepreneurial thinking, and inspire students to transform their ideas into impactful projects.

Our Principles
