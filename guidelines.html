04. PARTICIPATION GUIDELINES

Rules & Evaluation Criteria

Please review the guidelines carefully prior to registering your team and preparing project submissions.

Participant Guidelines

  • Participation: Solo or team participation is allowed.
  • Interdisciplinary Teams: Allowed only for team participation.
  • Team Composition: Teams must consist exclusively of male or female participants. Mixed-gender teams are not permitted.
Build your idea. Showcase your innovation. Make an impact.

Presentation & Pitch

  • 10 minutes live presentation + 5 minutes technical Q&A with jury.
  • Working prototype demonstration is mandatory for hardware and software tracks.
  • Maintain high technical clarity, problem framing, and architecture diagrams.
